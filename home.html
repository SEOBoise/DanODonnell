01 / A practical starting point

Better business starts with better questions.

AI consulting connects artificial intelligence to a specific business need: a clearer decision, a more useful customer experience or a workflow that gives people their time back.

That starts with understanding how your business works. Where does work stall? Which tasks repeat? What information do people need? And where does human judgment matter most?

My work brings together search marketing, business strategy and AI implementation. Through Coffee Cup AI, I help companies find opportunities, test them in real workflows and build the skills to keep improving. The aim is useful change your team can understand, own and measure.

03 / What this looks like

Start with work
your team recognizes.

AI becomes easier to evaluate when the task, the handoff and the measure of success are concrete.

Illustrative workflow / Marketing & sales

Turn research into a better brief.

A marketing team can use AI to organize approved customer research, compare recurring themes and prepare a first brief. An experienced person checks the evidence, chooses the angle and approves the final direction.

What to measure

Time to an approved brief, factual corrections, review effort and whether the work answers a real customer need.

01Approved research and customer questions
02AI-assisted synthesis and first draft
03Human fact-checking and strategic judgment
04An approved brief, ready for the team
Illustrative workflow / Business operations

Give incoming work a clear next step.

An operations team can use AI to summarize a request, suggest a category and prepare the next action. People review exceptions and authorize consequential changes. Integration should preserve permissions and an audit trail.

What to measure

Routing accuracy, time to first action, exception rate and the total review time per completed request.

01A request enters an approved system
02AI suggests a summary and category
03A person checks exceptions and permissions
04The request reaches the right owner
Illustrative workflow / Team knowledge

Make trusted information easier to find.

An internal AI assistant can help employees retrieve information from approved documentation. Answers should point to their sources, respect access controls and make uncertainty visible. An accountable person maintains the knowledge base.

What to measure

Answer accuracy, source quality, time spent searching and the frequency of questions that need human help.

01A question from an authorized employee
02Retrieval from approved, current documents
03An answer with sources and limitations
04Verification or escalation to a person

Examples illustrate an approach. They are not client case studies or promised results.

04 / The consulting process

A clear path.
A measurable decision.

Every engagement begins with your situation. The scope can vary; the discipline stays consistent.

01

Understand the work

Talk with the people closest to the process. Map the task, current tools, information, constraints and business goal. Record a baseline before introducing change.

OUTCOME / A shared problem definition
02

Choose a useful pilot

Compare opportunities by expected value, effort and risk. Select a manageable use case. Agree on the data, responsibilities, cost and success criteria.

OUTCOME / A focused plan to test
03

Build, test & teach

Try the workflow with representative work. Evaluate errors and edge cases. Train the people using it and document the decisions that require human approval.

OUTCOME / Evidence from real practice
04

Measure & improve

Compare results with the baseline, including correction time and total cost. Decide whether to expand, revise or stop. Assign ownership for ongoing monitoring.

OUTCOME / An informed next step

Measure value after the human review.

A fast first draft is only part of the story. AI return on investment should reflect the complete workflow: preparation, software, integration, training, checking and maintenance. Compare the final result with the work it replaces.

09 / Before we talk

A few useful
starting questions.

Clarity about the work comes before choosing an AI platform or signing up for more software.

Is AI consulting right for a smaller business?

It can be, when there is a recurring business problem worth solving. A smaller company can begin with one workflow, a small group and a clear measure of success. The first step is to assess whether AI adds enough value to justify its cost and complexity.

What should I bring to an initial conversation?

Bring one or two examples of work that takes too long, creates avoidable errors or limits growth. It helps to know who performs the task, how often it happens, which systems are involved and what a better outcome would look like. Please keep confidential customer or employee data out of an initial email.

How are cost and timing determined?

Scope depends on your goals, data readiness, integrations, training needs and the level of ongoing support. A focused review is different from a multi-department implementation. We can define the deliverables, responsibilities, schedule and commercial terms once the problem is clear.

Do we need a custom AI model?

Not necessarily. An existing tool, a better knowledge base or a simpler process may address the need. The choice should follow an evaluation of accuracy, privacy, integration, maintenance and total cost. Custom development should have a clear business reason.
