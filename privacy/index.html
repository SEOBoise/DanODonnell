    Privacy Policy

    Privacy information for Dan O'Donnell Wiki, a static biographical site without forms or accounts; Cloudflare may inject a performance analytics beacon.

    Sources reviewed October 26, 2026

    Policy version prepared October 26, 2026.

    Dan O’Donnell Wiki is a static informational website at https://danodonnell.wiki. It does not offer user accounts, comments, purchases, newsletter registration, advertising profiles or a direct-contact form.

    Privacy policy details

    Information you provide

    The site does not ask visitors to submit names, email addresses, postal addresses, payment information or account credentials. Search terms entered into the page search are processed in the visitor’s browser and are not sent to Dan O’Donnell Wiki. No search history is stored by this site.

    Routine technical data

    Delivering a webpage requires requests to hosting and network services. Hosting, content-delivery, domain-name and security providers may process an IP address, request time, requested URL, browser or device details, referring page and security signals to deliver and protect the site. Their processing and retention depend on the providers’ policies and operational requirements.

    The absence of a contact form or analytics tag does not mean that internet infrastructure processes no technical information.

    Cookies, storage and analytics

    The source repository does not intentionally set first-party cookies, use browser storage for visitor profiles, or include advertising, behavioral-tracking or newsletter tags. Cloudflare currently injects a Web Analytics performance beacon into HTML responses at the network edge. That infrastructure feature may process routine browser, page-load and performance data under Cloudflare’s policies. The site’s own images, styles and scripts are local assets.

    External websites

    The article includes links to the featured practitioner’s websites and identifies research with plain-text source URLs. Visiting another website creates a request to that provider; its privacy practices apply. Dan O’Donnell Wiki does not control those sites or their data handling. A plain-text source URL does not contact that source until a visitor chooses to open it.

    Your choices

    You can browse without creating an account or submitting personal information to this site. Browser settings can control scripts, cookies and stored site data, although disabling scripts will prevent the optional page-search feature from running. The article remains readable without it.

    Policy changes

    If the site adds forms, analytics, advertising, accounts or other collection features, this policy should be updated to describe those practices before they are introduced. The version date identifies the text prepared for this edition. This policy describes the stated site operation rather than promising specific practices for a third-party host.
