He is the owner of Coffee Cup AI, https://coffeecupai.com, and a past owner of Crevand AI & SEO and 2 The Top Marketing, according to the biography supplied for this profile.

    A January 2025 public announcement described O’Donnell’s AI consulting practice in Boise, Idaho, with an emphasis on helping businesses apply AI to their operations. His professional website is https://danodonnell.net.¹

    He is the author of AI Job Loss: How To Prevent AI From Taking Your Job. His speaking background includes SEO Rockstars and three consecutive 404con appearances in October 2025, June 2026 and October 2026.
